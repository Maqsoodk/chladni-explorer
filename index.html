About the model

The nodal figures use analytical modes of an ideal 30 cm, 1 mm steel square plate with simply supported boundaries. Sand motion is an illustrative, phenomenological transport simulation with the outer-edge attractor suppressed for visual clarity. Historical Chladni experiments generally use different supports and freer edges. Displayed frequencies are theoretical, not measured.
